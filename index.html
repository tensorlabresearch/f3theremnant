Your first workout

Just show up.
We’ll handle the rest.

  1. Pick a workoutChoose any location and arrive 10 minutes early.
  2. Bring the basicsWorkout clothes, water, gloves, and a headlamp for dark mornings.
  3. Go at your paceEvery exercise can be modified. You are never left behind.
  4. Earn your F3 nameStick around afterward. The group will welcome you properly.

The F3 Mission

The Mission of F3 is to plant, grow, and serve small workout groups for men for the invigoration of male community leadership
